# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

People in Mumbai, especially Bandra, who are deciding whether Mokai feels like a place they want to visit, linger in, and share. They are browsing for atmosphere as much as practical details such as the menu, spaces, location, hours, and contact information.

## Product Purpose

The site introduces Mokai as a designed pause rather than a transactional coffee stop. It should make the visitor feel the relationship between food, matcha, interiors, and participation, then give them a clear path to visit.

## Brand Personality

Warm, playful, tactile. The voice is editorial and witty, with Japanese restraint interrupted by Harajuku colour, Mumbai energy, and a slower rhythm. The experience should feel composed, social, and a little unexpected.

## Anti-references

Do not look like a generic SaaS landing page, a sterile luxury hospitality site, a template cafe website, or a technology demo where decorative 3D overwhelms the place and the brand. Avoid anonymous beige minimalism, generic gradients, and visual polish that loses the imperfect material character of Mokai.

## Design Principles

- Show the atmosphere before asking for a transaction.
- Let calm and playful interruption coexist.
- Treat food, space, and ritual as one experience.
- Use real place imagery and tactile graphic cues to make the brand specific.
- Keep navigation and practical visit details clear beneath the art direction.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support keyboard navigation, visible focus states, readable contrast, meaningful image alt text, and a reduced-motion experience. Do not make meaning depend on colour or animation, and keep the main visit path usable on small screens and slower devices.
